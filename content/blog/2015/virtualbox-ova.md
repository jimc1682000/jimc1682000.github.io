一、選擇「匯入應用裝置」  

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgx2oVoBEP-dWKHoW2XxB0Dax0pPtz7FzUo4UgUrWSaKVUsw28umVEiTTYC2GBAFNONgqJb7lyFSE2r2NF3K8y-h0V8L7v1nXrUUvFDpd4ht5PEJLz8Kd6GLEWn69gxZ59doBZs11k7TNJl/s640/OVA-20150913-005.png)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgx2oVoBEP-dWKHoW2XxB0Dax0pPtz7FzUo4UgUrWSaKVUsw28umVEiTTYC2GBAFNONgqJb7lyFSE2r2NF3K8y-h0V8L7v1nXrUUvFDpd4ht5PEJLz8Kd6GLEWn69gxZ59doBZs11k7TNJl/s1600/OVA-20150913-005.png)

  
二、找到檔案位置，進行匯入  

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgAuCBQ_Xma_ngTbR3SMgc9IkiX6utlQBg0mw5DVIFyyJ9x_9yLuYvC35MSLDnjJ-Ty28NcSEsK5iWCjUeZWZU-ugPOcS3idYd2ohrTe0bNn33c2qqau3t0AbhOSNMPd6OECrEidAEZDESh/s400/OVA-20150913-006.png)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgAuCBQ_Xma_ngTbR3SMgc9IkiX6utlQBg0mw5DVIFyyJ9x_9yLuYvC35MSLDnjJ-Ty28NcSEsK5iWCjUeZWZU-ugPOcS3idYd2ohrTe0bNn33c2qqau3t0AbhOSNMPd6OECrEidAEZDESh/s1600/OVA-20150913-006.png)
