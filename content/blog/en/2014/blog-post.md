First, let me explain how to use the tuning pack:  
1. Pick up the tuning pack (obviously).  
2. Please note!! It is not a damping pad, even though it looks a lot like one, orz.  
3. Since the tuning pack is meant to bring gear to an "electrically neutral" state, it works best placed where there's power flowing. I'd recommend putting it above/below a transformer or a DAC/headphone amp—the key is just not to crush it hard.  
That's also why it has no real effect on passive speakers…  
In my case, I put it on top of my Supplier HD + Spitfire HD + Beyond HD.  
4. Lay it as flat as possible so the contact area between the pack and the gear is as large as possible.  
PS: Since I happen to have a Xiaomin 3 on hand, I asked about the recommended operating temperature… Mr. Chen said the main concern is the fabric scorching, so he doesn't recommend going above 80°C. My Xiaomin 3 runs at around 50–60°C, which is fine, and it's worked out well for me after using it. If you're worried, you might want to test a bit more.  
5. Wait…  
The whole electrical-neutralizing process takes 30 minutes at the fastest and 2 hours at the slowest, and the sound is at its best by then.  
Of course you can still listen during this period; it just feels weird because the sound keeps changing, XD  
6. Enjoy happily~~
