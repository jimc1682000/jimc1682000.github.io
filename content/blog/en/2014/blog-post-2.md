Haha, XDD blogs don't feel as popular as they used to, XDD  
Social networks like FB have taken over instead, but I still think  
that if you have something long to say, a blog is still the more proper place for it, XD  
Besides, a lot of the master-level folks and seriously skilled experts I know all keep their own blogs.  
On one hand it lets them share their knowledge with everyone; on the other, it works as a personal memory-organizing database, XD  
After enough time passes, you forget how to use some things yourself, and you can dig the info back out of this database. You can also watch your own growth over the years through it, which is a lot of fun. Writing posts does take some time, but I think carving out a bit of my own words and the tracks of a life lived, over this long river of time, is actually a pretty nice thing to do. I hope I can keep this blog going with persistence, ^^  
  
On My Jimmy City, I'll mostly put up my many thoughts on using 3C gadgets, with audio gear, headphones, and computers making up the bulk of it. There will also be tutorials for good software, plus some server and security-related setups or how-tos; then there's the eating, drinking, wandering, and sightseeing sharing; or my stance and views on current events. This last part I might encrypt or something, with the password made public, XD The point is I'm worried people with different positions or views might not be too happy seeing this stuff, so anyone interested can open it up themselves… Basically, I still hope My Jimmy City can be a blog that everyone finds fun, fresh, and informative, ~  
  
So, My Jimmy City~  
Is~ now~ o~pen!
